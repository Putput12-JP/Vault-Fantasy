Minutes Lab: four ways to tweak a rotation

The same New York Knicks projection (season-start numbers from the app, scaled to a clean 240 minutes) in four layouts. Every one is live: move minutes and the stats move with them. Pick the one, or mix pieces.

VARIANT 1

Depth chart

Starters up top as big cards, the bench as slim rows. Reads like a lineup card, so it is the easiest to scan.

VARIANT 2

Mixer

The 240-minute bar is the control. Drag a divider and minutes move between the two neighbours, so the total never changes.

VARIANT 3

Fast list

Dense rows for power users: type a number or nudge it, see the change against the projection tick, and read every stat across.

VARIANT 4

Scenarios first

Start from a situation, then fine-tune. One tap models a blowout, a rest night or a star out, and the impact panel says what moved.
