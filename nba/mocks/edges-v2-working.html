The rule. A signal with a losing live record on 100 or more settled bets is hidden and never staked, whatever last season's backtest said. A signal under 100 settled is kept and labelled Collecting; it is shown, but not staked until it reaches 100 and holds up.
Realistic edge is the promised edge after the backtest haircut. Stake is fractional Kelly on that number, capped per play and per game.
Hidden plays are still logged and settled in the Track Record, so a signal that turns around comes back on its own.
