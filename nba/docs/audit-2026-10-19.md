# Vault NBA audit and change plan (2026-10-19)

Opening night is Oct 20. Method: read the NBA docs, code and live board (`nba-live` board.json, 7 minutes old at read time), then scanned
competitors, X, GitHub, news and betting sites. **Not read:** the live shadow ledger (`track.json`, `bets/`), per the experiment-log rule.
Reddit could not be searched from this environment (blocked), so nothing below claims a Reddit view.

## 1. Where Vault already leads

| Area | Vault | Typical competitor |
|---|---|---|
| Honesty | Pre-registered tests, 68-cell multiplicity count, "bias alone" baseline, realistic edge beside promised edge | Self-reported ROI (PropsBot "25.1% on 88k picks"), A+ to F grades, "20%+ edge = strong" with no track record |
| Venues | Kalshi, Novig, Polymarket, Pinnacle, DraftKings, PrizePicks, Underdog, Sleeper, one board | One or two books, or books only |
| Timing | Injury Wire (per-venue reaction clock), lineup Expected to Confirmed, news-lag edge types | Alerts with no measured lag |
| Minutes | Tonight page: minutes with parts, range, market-implied minutes. PropsMadness just shipped "Expected Minutes" as a headline feature | Single minutes number, no market cross-check |
| Record | Shadow ledger over every market, CLV, cube slicing | None audited |

The market is crowded with "AI picks". Nobody else publishes a record that survives scrutiny. That is the product; the plan below adds
coverage and decision speed around it, not another model.

## 2. Gaps found (verified in the repo or the live board unless marked)

### A. Feed gaps on the board (fix before or at opening night)
Live board `skipped` labels, 2026-10-19:
- **Sleeper combos dropped: 406 lines.** Sleeper sends `points_and_rebounds` (154), `rebounds_and_assists` (130), `points_and_assists` (122).
  `SL_STAT` in `scripts/prop_board.py:85` expects `pts_reb`, `reb_ast`, `pts_ast`. A one-line alias fixes it. Sleeper is the user's own platform.
- **Double-double / triple-double: about 175 lines** (PrizePicks 122 + 13, Underdog 13, Sleeper 26). Roadmap item 6 said "needs a real feed";
  PrizePicks, Underdog and Sleeper now post them. Flat payout means a price exists for pick'em.
- **First-quarter points / rebounds / assists: about 300 lines** (Underdog `period_1_*` 173, Sleeper `first_qtr_*` 123). Quarter props
  have no model, so these need the v3 shape plus a 1Q minutes share, display only until tested.
- **Fantasy Score (92) and Dunks (20)** on PrizePicks. Low value; leave unmapped on purpose and say so.
- **Kalshi has 92 prop rungs on the board against 1,102 DraftKings rows.** Preseason, so expected. It is the only venue with a validated edge,
  so thin Kalshi listings cap how often the one GO cell can fire. Watch it nightly for the first week.

### B. Market-structure change (news, Oct 16 2026, unofficial)
ESPN and Covers report the NBA told sportsbooks to drop: **same-player under parlays, opening-tip bets, "player to miss a specific shot",
and unders on players expected under 12 minutes**. Not confirmed as implemented by FanDuel or DraftKings, effective dates unstated. Prediction
markets are outside it; the league asked the CFTC for a framework (regulatory risk for the Kalshi leg).
Implications to plan for, none yet measured:
1. Books may post **over-only** lines for low-minute players. Pricing must accept one-sided markets (this is roadmap Phase 0.2, ESPN milestone ladders).
2. Under parlay demand drops. Vault's one repeatable finding is that **overs are overpriced**. If books shade unders after the change, the
   bias could narrow. Track the overs bias weekly from night one (differentiation.md already says stop the moment it closes).
3. Injury reports now update every 15 minutes and teams must **resubmit on game days** (11am to 1pm local, 8 to 10am for tips at 5pm or
   earlier; skipped on the second night of a back-to-back). That is a predictable news window per team.

### E. Looked at and rejected
- More model variants. The log says accuracy is not the edge, and v3 shape, minutes GBM and Kalman rates all tied once blended with a price.
- 10,000-simulation "edges" (FTN), A+ to F grades, AI pick lists. Our own simulation challenger was NO-GO as a probability engine.
- SGP correlation builders (Outlier). The same-player-under parlay ban shrinks that market; our pick'em correlation work covers the user's apps.
- Paid feeds. Free-only rule stands. The Odds API free tier is 500 credits a month and props cost credits per market and region, so it adds nothing over what the recorder already reads.
- Copying DARKO or Pelton minutes. Cannot be pulled free in a verified way; the minutes GBM is already a shadow against v2.

## 3. Plan

Principle from `update-plan.md` holds: no signal is promoted here. Every new input starts display-only or as a pre-registered test row in
`experiment-log.md` before anyone reads results. CLAUDE.md applies: branch plus pull request, changelog entry for anything players see, never push main.

### Phase A: before opening night (today, small)
1. **Alias the Sleeper combo labels** (`SL_STAT`). Result: 406 lines appear on the board. Old number 0, new number about 406.
2. **Map double-double / triple-double** as a pick'em-only market (flat payout, no model price shown until a real probability exists from the Game Simulation's joint draws). Display the lines now, price them after a test.
3. **Map first-quarter points / rebounds / assists** as display-only pick'em lines, labelled "no Vault number".
4. **One-sided market check** on `check_board.py` the hour before the first tip: DK over-only rows, ESPN milestone ladders (Phase 0.2).
5. Add **report-window countdown** to Tonight: per team, "report due by" from the resubmission rule (confirm the rule text on the first game day).

### Phase B: first two weeks (measure, then show)
1. **Questionable-mixture pricing and a "report pending" flag** (C1). Prop with a Questionable teammate shows a range, not one edge. Pre-register the test: does mixture pricing reduce miss on rung log loss for those games.
2. **Overs-bias tracker plus always-under baseline** in the ledger (D5). Weekly line on Track Record. Records the effect of the NBA's market change (B2).
3. **Check my lean** v1 (D1), singles first. Reuses `priceRow`, the Tonight flags and the Pairs correlation. Adds the one number the page lacks for this user: "when Vault passed a lean like this, here is the record (n)".
4. **Building-the-record state** on Track Record, Edges, Tonight (D4).
5. **Per-venue report-window reaction clock**: the Injury Wire already measures lag; add "inside a resubmission window" as a cut.

### Phase C: November (pre-registered tests, then features)
1. **Rest and travel features** (C3) on minutes and game total. **Sit prior** (C2) on minutes. Both written into `experiment-log.md` first, tested on the 2026-27 live data and the held-out parts of 2025-26.
2. **Officials context** (C4): show the crew and its foul tendency on Tonight. Test FTA, PF and totals only after 150 games.
3. **Live bet tracker** (D2) with the Game Simulation's remaining-game chance. Spike first: confirm the live box score endpoint answers from the user's browser and from CI.
4. **Post-game "why"** on every logged bet (D3).
5. **Pick'em of the night + weekly recap** (roadmap 5), singles only.
6. Context layer from the CourtAI teardown (diet, defense sheet, with n and thin-sample flags), still no edge claim.

### Phase D: later, only if data supports
- First-quarter prop model (needs 1Q box scores; hoopR carries quarter lines, to confirm).
- Beat-writer note layer (C6) once typed notes show whether they lead the lineup feed.
- Copula A2 holdout (Nov 9) and minutes shock (Nov 16) stay on schedule.

## 4. Risks and unknowns
- The Oct 16 restrictions are from unnamed-source reports. If books do not implement them, B1 to B3 are moot.
- The Kalshi edge rests on thin books (median 105 contracts per 30 minutes). Opening-night depth is unmeasured.
- Live endpoints: the NBA stats API blocks cloud IPs (known). The live data JSON host is not yet tested from CI.
- Nothing here was backtested. Every "gap" above is a coverage or workflow gap; any claim about edge is a hypothesis with a test attached.
