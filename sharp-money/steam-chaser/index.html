How this works
  • A reference venue moved a player prop: Pinnacle (a line or 5 points of no-vig chance), Novig (5 points at one strike), heavy Kalshi money, or sharp Polymarket accounts.
  • A slower book or pick'em app still holds the old number. EV is that book's price against the reference's no-vig chance when it moved, at the same line only. A different line shows as a line gap, with no EV.
  • Agreement is how many different reference venues moved the same side within an hour. It is context, not an agree total.
  • Every move is logged and graded against Pinnacle's close and the box score. The thresholds are not fitted. Novig prop history began 2026-10-30.
