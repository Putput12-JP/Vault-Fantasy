Sharp Money, with an Apple Sports layout

Three screens as a phone mock. Numbers are real, taken from the Sharp Money data file (generated ). Nothing here is shipped. Sharp money stays context only, so there is no "agree" count anywhere.

What this borrows from Apple Sports

  • Pure black canvas with rounded dark cards, no borders, one big title.
  • Each game is two team rows with a colored team badge. Big condensed numerals carry the key number on the right.
  • Game page opens with a gradient built from the two team colors, then translucent platters over it.
  • Alerts look like Lock Screen cards: a team color stripe, one line of text, one big number.
  • One tap target per game. Nothing to drill into on the board itself.

What stays Vault

  • Every sharp reading is labeled context only. No agree total, no bet signal.
  • Team badges use the stored team color and abbreviation (no logos are stored).
  • Lines, flow, Kalshi and Polymarket prices, and the Vault line are all stored values. The model rows say they are context.

Not copied

  • No Apple assets, logos, icons or fonts. The condensed numerals use Barlow Condensed as a stand in.
