Subject: Permission request: displaying odds from the SmartStake API in Vault Fantasy

Hi SmartStake team,

I'm the developer of Vault Fantasy (vaultfantasy.com), a free fantasy football and betting-context tool. We use your Odds API
under the early-access terms and we're recording NFL and NBA lines for internal research and backtesting only.

We would like written permission to display a small slice of that data in our line-movement chart:

- Which: NFL and NBA spread, total and moneyline for Circa and Bookmaker (books we cannot get elsewhere). Pregame only.
- How: a step chart per game showing when each book changed its line, with the book name. No raw feed, no bulk download, no API.
- Not: no resale, no redistribution, no bet links, and nothing offered as a data product or feed.
- Attribution: "Lines via SmartStake" with a link, on the chart, if you want it.
- Delay: we can show lines on a delay (for example 15 minutes) if you prefer.

Could you confirm in writing whether this is allowed, and under what conditions? If a paid or partner tier would cover it, we're
open to that too.

Thanks,
Jacob Putman
