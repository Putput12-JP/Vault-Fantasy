How this record works

  • Grades are set before kickoff from Vault's own projection, judged against the price of that side. Vault grades every prop it can model, including the ones it thinks you should avoid (D and F).
  • Results settle from ESPN and nflverse box scores, usually within minutes of the final whistle.
  • Return is measured at the closing price for the graded side. Your own price may be better or worse depending on when and where you bet.
  • Pushes are left out of win rate and return. Props with no closing price count in the record but not in return.
  • This is a model's record, not betting advice. Bet only what you can afford to lose.
