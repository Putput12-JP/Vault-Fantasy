Note on format: per the user's standing rule, no em dashes are used. Each finding ends with its source in brackets.

## 1. Founding story, founders, funding, headcount, acquisitions

### Takeaway
OddsJam was founded in 2021 by Alex Monahan (ex-Susquehanna quant, Stanford) and Ankit Goyal, bootstrapped with roughly $20-25k and zero VC, bought OddsBoom in Aug 2022, and was sold to Gambling.com Group (Nasdaq: GAMB) for up to $160M ($80M upfront) in a deal that closed Jan 1, 2025. At sale it did about $26M revenue and $12M adjusted EBITDA (about 46% margin) with only about 30-35 employees.

### Cited Findings
- Founders: Ankit Goyal and Alex Monahan; CEO of parent Odds Holdings at sale was Matt Restivo. ([Gambling.com Group press release, SEC exhibit 99.1, Dec 12 2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839799/000183979924000084/exhibit991-pressreleasexde.htm))
- Founded in 2021; Goyal and Monahan attended the same Virginia high school and college; Monahan was previously a Wall Street quant trader. ([BettingStartups newsletter, Dec 2024](https://news.bettingstartups.com/p/oddsjam-acquired))
- Earnings+More describes the founders as "Stanford roommates" and says the platform was rebuilt three times in four years to cut latency. ([Earnings+More, "jam today"](https://earningsandmore.substack.com/p/jam-today))
- Launched April 2021 during COVID lockdowns, self-funded, scaled to about $20M revenue; YouTube betting-strategy content named as key growth driver. ([Entrepreneurs on Fire episode, Jul 10 2025](https://www.eofire.com/podcast/alexmonahan/))
- Monahan's background: Stanford, then quant trader at Susquehanna Financial Group trading index derivatives; personal investment $20k-25k, $0 VC; early revenue path $5k to $10k to $20k per month. ([The Startup Storys, Mar 2026, secondary/aggregator](https://www.thestartupstorys.com/2026/03/alex-monahans-oddsjam-story.html)). CONFLICT: this source calls Monahan a "solo founder", which contradicts the SEC press release naming Goyal as co-founder. Treat "solo founder" as wrong.
- No external funding rounds recorded. ([Tracxn profile](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/oddsjam/__YVZl2cTvMbk8PkYfyaqzfyHqYfMlFSJpVttQhUrqStA); [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/oddsjam))
- Company was profitable by early 2023, giving it flexibility on fundraising; had acquired "Odds Boom" the prior year. ([BettingStartups Podcast Ep. 61, Mar 1 2023](https://www.bettingstartups.com/podcast/episode-61-oddsjam))
- Aug 2022: OddsJam acquired odds comparison site OddsBoom; founder Mark Knight joined as Head of Product; OddsJam had a 10-person editorial team at the time. ([Gaming Americas, Aug 4 2022](https://gamingamericas.com/latest-news/2022/08/04/88025/oddsjam-acquires-oddsboom/); [SBC Americas, Aug 5 2022](https://sbcamericas.com/2022/08/05/oddsjam-oddsboost-odds-comparison-acquisition/); [Gambling Insider](https://www.gamblinginsider.com/news/17489/affiliate-oddsjam-acquires-oddsboom-adds-founder-to-executive-team))
- Exit terms: $80M initial ($70M cash + $10M GAMB stock) plus up to $80M contingent; full earnout required Odds Holdings' adjusted EBITDA to at least double in FY2026 vs 2024; financed by a $100M Wells Fargo term loan/revolver; closed Jan 1, 2025. ([SEC exhibit 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839799/000183979924000084/exhibit991-pressreleasexde.htm); [Business Wire close, Jan 2 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250102618058/en/Gambling.com-Group-Completes-Acquisition-of-Odds-Holdings-Inc.-Parent-Company-of-OddsJam))
- 2024 financials (pre-close guidance): revenue about $26M, adjusted EBITDA about $12M; 2025 adjusted EBITDA expected to grow at least 20%. ([SEC exhibit 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839799/000183979924000084/exhibit991-pressreleasexde.htm))

([NEXT.io](https://next.io/news/investment/gambling-com-amends-oddsjam-earnout-structure/); [SEC 6-K amendment](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839799/000162828025059071/form6-kxamendmenttoagreeme.htm))

## 2. Business model: subscriptions, affiliate, B2B data (OpticOdds), media

### Takeaway
OddsJam is primarily a subscription software business (the acquirer explicitly bought it to add forecastable recurring revenue), with a B2B data arm (OpticOdds) selling low-latency odds feeds to sportsbooks and media. It runs a creator affiliate program that pays out on its own subscriptions, rather than depending mainly on sportsbook CPA.

### Cited Findings
- Earnings+More characterizes OddsJam's revenue as subscription-based and independent of the traditional gambling affiliate business; GAMB CEO Charles Gillespie cited "diversification" and said subscription businesses are "much easier to forecast". Macquarie called it a "best-in-class tech platform"; Jefferies called it an "appropriate acquisition". ([Earnings+More](https://earningsandmore.substack.com/p/jam-today))
- The deal added "complementary recurring revenue streams from consumer subscription products and enterprise clients". ([iGaming Business](https://igamingbusiness.com/strategy/ma/gambling-com-group-acquire-oddsjam/); [Business Wire, Dec 12 2024](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241212903186/en/Gambling.com-Group-Enters-into-Definitive-Agreement-to-Acquire-Odds-Holdings-Parent-Company-of-OddsJam-a-Fast-Growing-Technology-Platform-for-Real-time-Odds-Data-in-Accretive-Transaction))
- Post-acquisition at GAMB: sports data services revenue up 405% YoY to $9.9M in Q1 2025, "primarily due to" OddsJam and OpticOdds; recurring subscription revenue was 24% of GAMB total revenue in Q1 2025 and Q3 2025. ([search summary of GAMB Q1/Q3 2025 releases](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839799/000183979925000044/gamb-q12025xpressrelease.htm); [Q3 2025 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839799/000183979925000075/gamb-q32025xpressrelease.htm); [NEXT.io Q1 2025](https://next.io/news/results/gambling-com-group-q1-2025-results/))
- GAMB now reports a separate "Data" segment alongside "Marketing"; OpticOdds is a consolidated entity. ([GAMB 20-F FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839799/000183979926000048/gamb-20251231.htm))
- OpticOdds (B2B): aggregates 200+ sportsbooks into one feed, claims 200+ customers including BetMGM, Kambi and Entain; OddsJam's developer API is powered by OpticOdds. ([OpticOdds homepage](https://opticodds.com/); [OpticOdds API page](https://opticodds.com/sports-betting-api); [OddsPapi comparison 2026](https://oddspapi.io/blog/best-odds-apis-2026-comparison/))
- Operator-focused providers (OddsJam, OpticOdds, Sportradar) gate pricing behind sales. ([OddsPapi](https://oddspapi.io/blog/best-odds-apis-2026-comparison/))
- Consumer pricing: RotoWire (Jan 2026) lists Fantasy plan $1.97/day monthly ($1.32/day annual), Gold $6.60/day monthly ($5.40/day annual), plus Platinum, with a 7-day free trial. ([RotoWire review, Jan 8 2026](https://www.rotowire.com/betting/oddsjam-review)). Other reviews put Gold at about $199-249/month and Platinum about $500/month (about $400/mo annual). ([ArbBets pricing](https://getarbitragebets.com/blog/oddsjam-pricing); [Bet Hero alternatives](https://betherosports.com/blog/oddsjam-alternative)). Pricing varies by source and date; treat ranges as approximate.
- Product surface: positive EV tool, arbitrage, promo converter (claims 95-98% bonus conversion efficiency), low-hold, middles, fantasy optimizer/scores/middles for PrizePicks and Underdog, one-click bet placement, bet tracker, line history, push alerts. ([RotoWire](https://www.rotowire.com/betting/oddsjam-review))
- DFS lineup optimization via a PrizePicks partnership was cited at the time of the deal. ([BettingStartups newsletter](https://news.bettingstartups.com/p/oddsjam-acquired))
- Creator affiliate program: free, uncapped commissions, promo-code based; "the average partner" posts picks and analytics using OddsJam data on YouTube, X, Reddit, TikTok, Instagram, Threads; Ambassadors@OddsJam.com for coaching. One network lists 34% commission. ([OddsJam affiliate program via search](https://oddsjam.com/affiliate); [Tapfiliate portal](https://oddsjam.tapfiliate.com/); [Yazing listing](https://yazing.com/affiliate-programs/oddsjam-affiliate-program))
- Media arm: had a 10-person editorial team by Aug 2022. ([SBC Americas](https://sbcamericas.com/2022/08/05/oddsjam-oddsboost-odds-comparison-acquisition/))

### Inferences
- The "affiliate vs teaching users to beat books" tension is softened by the model: the main money is the user's subscription, not sportsbook CPA, so OddsJam can openly sell edge. Its buyer, an affiliate company, bought it precisely because it is NOT affiliate revenue. Sportsbook-side revenue instead comes through B2B (OpticOdds selling the same data pipe to operators like BetMGM/Entain). One pipe, two customers.
- The per-day price framing ($1.97/day) is a deliberate anchoring tactic for a high-ticket product.
- 46% EBITDA margin shows the infrastructure cost is manageable once built; data is reused across B2C and B2B.

### Gaps
- Could not verify whether OddsJam earns meaningful sportsbook CPA/rev-share today; no source broke out affiliate revenue.
- Could not load oddsjam.com/affiliate directly (403), so commission % and recurring terms are from third-party listings.
- OpticOdds pricing and customer count beyond the "200+" marketing claim are not public.

## 3. Growth and acquisition playbook

### Takeaway
Growth came overwhelmingly from founder-led free content (YouTube 1-3 videos a day showing real bets and product workflows, then TikTok/X/Twitch/Instagram, plus late-night live streams), backed by ROI-gated influencer promo codes, a creator affiliate program, a free trial, and free calculators for SEO. Today most traffic is direct (brand), a sign the content built a habit.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube was the primary growth engine: 1-3 videos daily showing real bets and product workflows, later expanded to TikTok, X, Twitch, Instagram; the first 5-minute video took two weeks to record; influencer spend tracked strictly by promo code/affiliate link and continued only if ROI exceeded cost; power users hired from the customer inbox (e.g., a former Illinois public school teacher). ([The Startup Storys, secondary](https://www.thestartupstorys.com/2026/03/alex-monahans-oddsjam-story.html))
- Monahan: "If you do a lot of good free content...it becomes hard not to see the value"; he still live streams bets late at night; content is education plus entertainment to build community. ([BettingStartups, May 2025](https://news.bettingstartups.com/p/oddsjam-s-alex-monahan-reflects-on-160m-exit))
- "Audience growth of educational content" was the stated primary focus in 2023. ([BettingStartups Podcast Ep. 61](https://www.bettingstartups.com/podcast/episode-61-oddsjam))
- Positioning line used widely: "the Bloomberg Terminal of sports betting"; in 2023 also "the home page of sports betting". ([EOFire](https://www.eofire.com/podcast/alexmonahan/); [BettingStartups Ep. 61](https://www.bettingstartups.com/podcast/episode-61-oddsjam))
- Company YouTube channel: "OddsJam: Sports Betting, Free Picks & Best Bets"; Monahan also runs a personal channel. ([OddsJam YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC3o3cUNVrcwrEE6B9CNGDbw/community); [Alex Monahan YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC3wc0NoEIsli3-dwkpb6rdQ/community))
- SEO: free "parlay calculator" page ranks about #9 for a 60,500/mo keyword; branded "oddsjam" (18,100/mo) and "odds jam" (2,400/mo) are top organic terms. ([Semrush overview, Aug 2026](https://www.semrush.com/website/oddsjam.com/overview/))
- 7-day free trial at signup. ([RotoWire](https://www.rotowire.com/betting/oddsjam-review))

### Inferences
- The playbook is "show the work": public, verifiable bets plus screen recordings of the tool double as proof, tutorial and ad at once. For a small analytics startup this is cheaper than paid acquisition and compounds.
- Creator affiliates repeat the founder's format at scale (post picks from the tool with a code), turning users into a distributed sales force.
- Free calculators are a real but secondary SEO wedge; organic search is only about 16% of traffic, so SEO was not the main engine.

### Gaps
- No public figures on YouTube/X/TikTok follower counts, Discord size, email list size, trial-to-paid conversion, or CAC.
- The "1-3 videos/day" and "influencers only if ROI positive" details come from an aggregator article, not a primary interview transcript; plausible but should be flagged.

## 4. Timing and market tailwinds

### Takeaway
OddsJam launched in April 2021, three years after PASPA's repeal (Murphy v. NCAA, May 2018), into peak US promo-bonus season when books were handing out large sign-up offers across many newly legal states; its promo converter, arbitrage and low-hold tools monetized exactly that. As promos faded, it leaned into DFS pick'em tools and exchanges.

### Cited Findings
- Founded post-Murphy v. NCAA (2018 legalization). ([The Startup Storys](https://www.thestartupstorys.com/2026/03/alex-monahans-oddsjam-story.html)); launched April 2021 during COVID lockdowns ([EOFire](https://www.eofire.com/podcast/alexmonahan/))
- Promo converter and low-hold tools exist to extract sportsbook bonuses and meet rollover requirements. ([RotoWire](https://www.rotowire.com/betting/oddsjam-review))
- Monahan (May 2025): traditional sportsbook promotions have diminished; exchange-style platforms like Novig and Sporttrade are the new opportunity for competitive bettors. ([BettingStartups](https://news.bettingstartups.com/p/oddsjam-s-alex-monahan-reflects-on-160m-exit))
- Fantasy plan and fantasy optimizer target PrizePicks/Underdog pick'em. ([RotoWire](https://www.rotowire.com/betting/oddsjam-review))

### Inferences
- The durable asset was the data pipe, not the promo era: when bonuses shrank, the same feed was repointed at pick'em, exchanges and B2B.

### Gaps
- No source quantified revenue by product (promo vs EV vs fantasy), so the promo-era contribution is inferred.

## 5. Moat

### Takeaway
The moat is low-latency data infrastructure across about 300 books, rebuilt three times for speed, reused for B2B, plus founder brand and educational content.

### Cited Findings
- Processes over 1 million requests per second and multiple terabytes of data per day across nearly 300 sportsbooks. ([SEC exhibit 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1839799/000183979924000084/exhibit991-pressreleasexde.htm))
- Rebuilt three times in four years for latency; Macquarie cited industry-leading latency. ([Earnings+More](https://earningsandmore.substack.com/p/jam-today))
- Consumer coverage listed as 100+ books (US majors plus 91+ international on Platinum). ([RotoWire](https://www.rotowire.com/betting/oddsjam-review)). Note the difference: "nearly 300" (company, B2B pipe) vs "100+" (consumer product as reviewed).
- Engagement signals: average visit 6:53 and 5.24 pages/visit per Similarweb, vs Unabated 3:55 and 4.56. ([Similarweb comparison, Aug 2026](https://www.similarweb.com/website/oddsjam.com/vs/unabated.com/))

### Inferences
- Sportsbooks limiting sharp accounts is an ongoing threat to the consumer value prop; B2B revenue from those same books hedges it.

### Gaps
- No independent latency benchmark found.

## 6. Competitors and relative pricing

### Takeaway
OddsJam sits at the premium end ($200-250/mo Gold, about $400-500/mo Platinum) with the broadest toolset; Unabated is comparable ($199/mo, $799 Concierge), while prop-focused tools like Outlier are about $99/mo.

### Cited Findings
- Unabated Premium $199/mo ($167 annual), Concierge $799/mo ($667 annual). ([XCLSV Unabated review 2026](https://xclsvmedia.com/unabated-review-2026-premium-sharp-bettor-tool-worth-it/))
- Outlier about $99/month (prop-focused). ([search summary citing Bet Hero/Outlier reviews](https://betherosports.com/blog/oddsjam-alternative); [BettingNews Outlier review](https://www.bettingnews.com/tools/outlier-bet-review/))
- Traffic: OddsJam global rank #67,132 vs Unabated #128,858; US sports betting category rank #78 vs #268 (Similarweb, Aug 2026). ([Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/oddsjam.com/vs/unabated.com/))

### Inferences
- A small entrant cannot out-cover OddsJam on books; the gap is in niche depth (fantasy-native, specific sport) at a lower price point, as Outlier did with props.
