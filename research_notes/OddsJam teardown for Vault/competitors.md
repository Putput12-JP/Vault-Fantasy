# OddsJam Competitive Landscape (as of Sept 2026)

Research date: 2026-09-26. Prices are "as reported" on the date of the cited page or review; many came from secondary review sites (XCLSV, BetSmart, OddsPlays, ArbBets, Picks & Parlays) because several vendor pricing pages are JS-rendered and did not return content to the fetcher (Unabated's `tools.unabated.com/pricing` and Action Network's `/pricing` returned no prices). Treat secondary-source prices as approximate and re-check before publishing.

## Q1. Per-competitor profile: pricing, features, coverage, monetization, traction

### Takeaway
The market splits into (a) sharp +EV/arb screens at $80 to $800/mo (OddsJam, Unabated, OddsShopper, Sharp App, AVO, Betstamp PRO), (b) prop research / "cheat sheet" apps at $15 to $30/mo (Outlier, Props.Cash, PropsMadness, PickFinder, BettingPros, Dimers, Rithmm), and (c) free bet trackers monetized by upsell or marketplaces (Pikkit, Juice Reel). All named competitors still exist in 2026; none found defunct. OddsJam itself is now owned by Gambling.com Group, an affiliate-marketing company.

## Q2. Which segments are crowded vs underserved

### Takeaway
Sharp +EV/arb screening across sportsbooks is saturated (a dozen tools, $80 to $800/mo) and prop hit-rate research at ~$20/mo is crowded. Underserved: genuinely free line shopping that is not delayed/capped, NFL fantasy-player-to-bettor bridges tied to a user's own league data, independently verifiable model track records, and pick'em help for casuals below $20/mo. Prediction-market tooling is emerging but mostly trader/arb oriented.

### Cited Findings
- Crowded sharp segment: OddsJam, Unabated, OddsShopper, Sharp App, AVO, Betstamp PRO, Bet Hero, Surebet, Betsniper all sell +EV/arb scanning — [ArbBets alternatives](https://getarbitragebets.com/blog/oddsjam-alternatives), [XCLSV affordable](https://xclsvmedia.com/best-affordable-ev-betting-tools-2026-cheap-oddsjam-alternatives/), [Betstamp](https://www.betstamp.com/comparison/oddsjam)
- Prop research at ~$20: Outlier $19.99, Props.Cash $19.99, PropsMadness $25, Action PRO $19.99 — sources above
- Free offerings are deliberately crippled: Sharp Lines free = 60-second delay, DK/FD only, 2% EV cap; AVO free = 3% profit cap — [XCLSV affordable](https://xclsvmedia.com/best-affordable-ev-betting-tools-2026-cheap-oddsjam-alternatives/)
- Review round-up explicitly notes no information on model track records or independent verification for any tool listed — [XCLSV affordable](https://xclsvmedia.com/best-affordable-ev-betting-tools-2026-cheap-oddsjam-alternatives/)
- Track records that do exist are self-reported (Sharp Football) or seller-level in a marketplace (Juice Reel "sell picks with proof") — [Sharp Football](https://www.sharpfootballanalysis.com/betting-fantasy-props-combo-packages/), [Juice Reel](https://www.juicereel.com/how-to-sell-picks)
- Pick'em EV tools cluster at $25 to $80/mo (PropsMadness $25, OddsJam Fantasy $59.99, Sharp App $79) — sources above
- OddsJam reportedly lacks prediction-market scanning; Outlier and OddsShopper added Kalshi; ArbBets/Stokastic target prediction-market traders — [ArbBets](https://getarbitragebets.com/blog/oddsjam-pricing), [Outlier App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/outlier-betting-data-tools/id6443885102), [OddsShopper](https://www.oddsshopper.com/subscribe), [RotoGrinders](https://rotogrinders.com/kalshi/predictions)

### Inferences
- Vault's open lanes: (1) free, undelayed NFL line shopping including DFS pick'em and Kalshi/Polymarket side by side; (2) a public, settled-from-data prop model record (almost no one publishes one that isn't self-graded marketing); (3) Sleeper-connected "your players' props" for fantasy managers; (4) casual pick'em guidance at $0 to $10.
- Competing head-on as a sharp +EV screen would mean fighting VC/acquirer-funded incumbents on book count and latency, where Vault is structurally weak.

### Gaps
- No survey or usage data quantifying demand for transparent track records or the fantasy-to-bettor funnel; inferred from absence of supply.

## Q3. Pricing ladder patterns

### Takeaway
The ladder is consistent: free (tracker/calculator/crippled scanner) -> $15 to $30/mo (prop research, AI picks, tracker Pro) -> $60 to $150/mo (pick'em optimizers, mid +EV) -> $199/mo (sharp standard) -> $400 to $800/mo (Platinum/Concierge). Annual discounts are steep (often 40 to 60%), and trials run 3 to 7 days.

### Cited Findings
- Free tier: Pikkit core, Juice Reel, BettingPros Prop Analyzer basics, PropsMadness free, AVO Basic, Sharp Lines, Crazy Ninja Devigger, The Odds API 500 credits — sources above
- $5 to $30: Crazy Ninja ~$5/mo; Action PRO $19.99; Outlier $19.99/$29.99; Props.Cash $19.99; OddsJam Trends $19.99; PropsMadness $25; BettingPros/Dimers/Rithmm Core/Pikkit Pro $29.99 — sources above
- $50 to $150: Rithmm Pro $49.99; OddsJam Fantasy $59.99; Sharp App $79; Outlier Pro $79.99; AVO $88 to $99; OddsShopper $99.95/$149.95; Rithmm Premium $99.99; Unabated Props+ ~$99 — sources above
- ~$199: Unabated Premium $199; OddsJam Sharp Money $199.99 — sources above
- $400+: OddsJam Platinum $499.99; Unabated Concierge $799; Sharp Football All-Access $329.99/4wks or $1,550/season — sources above
- Annual discounts: Outlier Pro $79.99/mo vs $359.99/yr (~62% off); BettingPros $29.99/mo vs $119.98/yr (~67% off); Dimers $29.99 vs $199.99/yr — sources above
- Trials: Outlier 7-day, OddsShopper 7-day, Rithmm 7-day, Props.Cash 7-day no card, Dimers 3-day; Unabated none (14-day refund) — sources above
- Week passes are common for NFL-season spikes: Action PRO $14.99/wk, Sharp Football $109.99/wk, AVO $22 day pass — sources above

### Inferences
- An NFL-only product has a seasonal shape; competitors monetize it with season passes and weekly passes (Props.Cash NBA Pass, Sharp Football season price). A Vault "NFL Season Pass" fits the established pattern and the existing monetization memo.
- The $0 to $10 band is thin (Crazy Ninja, BettingPros annual); a free core with a cheap season pass sits below nearly every competitor's entry price.
